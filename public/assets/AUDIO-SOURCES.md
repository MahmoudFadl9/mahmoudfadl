# Ambient audio sources

The site's background soundscape combines these two original, CC0-licensed recordings. Both are used at reduced volume and looped without alteration. They are paused when the site is muted or hidden and lowered during project/showreel playback.

- **Underwater Ambient Pad** — isaiah658, https://opengameart.org/content/underwater-ambient-pad — CC0 1.0; downloaded from https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Underwater-Ambient-Pad-isaiah658_0.ogg
- **Space Winds** — aquinn, https://opengameart.org/content/space-winds — CC0 1.0; downloaded from https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/space-wind_0.mp3
